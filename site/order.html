How to get your cake.

Three ways to get cake: same-day online, walk in, or a custom cake by phone. Here’s which one fits.

Same day, onlineWalk inCustom cake

A whole cake, today.

Our ordering site lists the cakes we have for today. Choose your flavor and size, pay online, and pick up at the shop.

  • 6″ cake · $56Feeds 8–10. Confetti, classic, chocolate ganache, strawberry, cookies & cream, vanilla almond, carrot, red velvet, and this season’s pumpkin spice.
  • 8″ cake · $68Feeds 16–20. Same flavors.
  • CutoffOrder by 6pm Monday–Saturday or 3pm Sunday. Orders placed after the cutoff are for pickup the next day the shop is open.
  • Designs varyColors, design and sprinkles change day to day. No returns or exchanges once cakes leave the shop.
