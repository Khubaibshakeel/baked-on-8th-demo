Baking on 8th since 2018

Leah and Adam Carmean are the husband-and-wife team behind Baked on 8th. They met at a large-scale bakery in 2008. In September 2017 they were both laid off within days of each other, and in 2018 they opened the bakery they had long planned.

Leah grew up in Memphis baking with her mom and grandmother, and wrote the first business plan in an entrepreneurship class at Belmont University. Adam trained in French classical cooking and has more than 20 years in culinary work.

The strawberry bread comes from Leah’s grandmother’s recipe.
